# CatBand visual assets

The active stage uses five generated transparent sprite sheets: drums.png, bass-four-tuners.png, guitar.png, keys-tabby.png, and vocals.png. Each sheet contains resting and playing poses. The cat cutout and real-instrument photomontage follow the reference images supplied by the project creator and were generated or revised with ImageGen for this demo.

The image sheets are part of this repository and are distributed under its MIT License. They are not stock photographs or original source meme images. Their performance poses illustrate rhythmic response; they do not claim anatomical accuracy or exact fingering.

bass.png, keys.png, and keys-v3.png are earlier drafts and are not needed by the app; they are intentionally not included in the public source tree.
